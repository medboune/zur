03 / Transparency

How we state condition

One letter in a model number or a different spindle size can change whether a machine fits your line. That’s why condition language is standardized and sourced.

Grade A

Used

In working condition as last operated. Operating history confirmed with the owner before quoting.

Grade B

Tested working

Powered up and function-tested before sale. Test report provided where one exists — ask for it.

Grade C

Refurbished

Restored or repaired to a stated standard. Refurbishment scope documented in the offer.

Grade D

As-is / for parts

Sold in current state with no operational warranty. Known history noted where available.

Condition statements come from the asset owner. We verify them while preparing your quote, and every offer records what was confirmed and on what date.
